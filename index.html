Who We Are

Lënster Lycée International School (LLIS) offers a unique learning environment in the East of Luxembourg, providing high-quality, free national and international education. Our European curriculum allows pupils to choose between English, French and German language sections — learning up to six languages, including Luxembourgish — while blending into an international community.

Our Values: Care, Create, Connect

Care

We empower students to impact the world positively by fostering empathy, open communication and a balanced approach to life — promoting trust, understanding and well-being.

Create

We nurture creativity and resilience, encouraging students to solve challenges imaginatively and contribute innovative solutions to global and community issues.

Connect

We build strong, collaborative relationships by celebrating diversity and multilingualism, creating a supportive, inclusive environment that values each individual's strengths.

Structure of Secondary Education

Secondary education in the European Section spans seven years across three cycles. The European Section runs alongside the Luxembourgish national programmes offered at LLIS — the table below shows how the two systems correspond.

CycleYearsComparable (national system)Focus
Observation CycleS1 – S37e – 5e (Cycle inférieur)Broad, balanced curriculum for every student
Pre-Orientation CycleS4 – S54e – 3e (Cycle supérieur)Core subjects with elective choices introduced
Orientation CycleS6 – S72e – 1re (Cycle supérieur)Specialisation and European Baccalaureate preparation

A defining feature of all three cycles is LLIS's commitment to multilingual education. Students are taught in English, French or German, follow Luxembourgish lessons, and develop fluency in several additional languages.

05

Languages at LLIS

Language learning is at the heart of the LLIS experience. Students become comfortable working across multiple languages — in language classes and in core academic subjects too.

L1

Mother Tongue

LLIS offers sections in English, French and German. Students whose first language does not have a section (SWALS) are placed in one of the three main sections with dedicated support.
L2

Vehicular Language

From S1, every student studies a first foreign language from the other section languages. From S3 it is also the language of instruction for Human Sciences and Ethics; from S4 for History, Geography and Economics. Many students reach C1 level by S7.
L3–L5

Additional Languages

A second foreign language (L3) from S1, a third (L4) from S4, and a fifth language (L5) from S6 — including Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Luxembourgish. Latin available from S2.
LanguageWhat students develop
Mother Tongue (L1)Advanced proficiency in English, French or German, enabling critical thinking and articulate communication.
First Foreign Language (L2)Bilingual fluency, using the second language for subjects like Human Sciences and fostering cross-disciplinary engagement.
Second Foreign Language (L3)Beginning in S1, students explore an additional language, broadening cultural horizons and communication skills.
Languages 4 and 5Other European languages can be chosen from S4 (comparable to 4e) or S6 (comparable to 2e).
LuxembourgishObligatory from S1 to S3; can be chosen as an option from S4 at beginner or advanced level.

The European Baccalaureate

The final mark is expressed as a percentage (50% = pass), officially recognised across all EU member states.

50%
Preliminary Mark
Teacher coursework grades combined with internal exams — B-tests throughout S6/S7, plus Pre-Baccalaureate exams in January of the final year.
35%
Written Exams
Five written exams including L1, L2, Mathematics and two elective subjects. Marked by the class teacher and an independent external examiner.
15%
Oral Exams
Three oral exams: two compulsory (L1, L2 or History/Geography) plus one elective. Students who studied Advanced Mathematics must take it as their third oral.

08

Orientation and Careers Guidance

LLIS supports students through the Cellule d'orientation et d'intégration (COI), part of the school's DEPS department. Orientation guidance becomes particularly active from S6 onwards, though students are welcome to make contact earlier.

What the COI offers
Orientation lessons integrated into the timetable for S3 students
Access to resources on studies, training and careers (digital and printed)
Workshops and information sessions on higher education and application processes
Participation in national events such as YEP!, Student Fair and Discovery Days
Personalised coaching and career interest profiling
Support for transitions, including re-orientation between school systems
University application support in Luxembourg and abroad
